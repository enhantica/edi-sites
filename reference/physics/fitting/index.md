# Fitting and statistics

Use the dedicated reference topics:

- [Rietveld method](https://easydiffraction.org/physics/rietveld/): calculated pattern, residual objective, joint and scan analyses.
- [Minimization and uncertainty](https://easydiffraction.org/physics/minimization/): LM strategy, constraints, covariance and correlations.
- [Reliability factors](https://easydiffraction.org/physics/reliability/): profile, conventional, Bragg and F factors and their conventions.

The [practical fit-quality guide](https://easydiffraction.org/user-guide/analysis-workflow/analysis/#fit-quality) explains how to assess these quantities together with the pattern and physical model.
